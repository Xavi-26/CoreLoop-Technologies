# CoreLoop Technologies

## Giving Technology a Second Life

CoreLoop Technologies is a South African technology recovery and e-waste solutions company focused on responsible electronic waste management, IT asset recovery, refurbishment, and secure data destruction.

## Services

- E-Waste Collection
- IT Asset Disposal (ITAD)
- Secure Data Destruction
- Computer & Laptop Refurbishment
- Electronics Recycling
- Corporate Equipment Buy-Back

## Accepted Equipment

- Laptops
- Desktop Computers
- Servers
- Network Equipment
- Printers
- UPS Systems
- Hard Drives & SSDs
- Cables & Electronic Accessories

## Mission

To reduce electronic waste through responsible collection, refurbishment, reuse, and recycling while helping businesses and individuals dispose of technology securely and sustainably.
