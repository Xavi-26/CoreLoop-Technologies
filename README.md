# CoreLoop Technologies

## Giving Technology a Second Life

CoreLoop Technologies is a South African technology recovery and e-waste solutions company specializing in responsible electronic waste management, IT asset disposition (ITAD), refurbishment, secure data destruction, and circular economy solutions.

Our goal is to extend the lifecycle of technology, recover valuable resources, and reduce the environmental impact of electronic waste through sustainable practices.

---

## Services

- E-Waste Collection
- IT Asset Disposition (ITAD)
- Secure Data Destruction
- Computer & Laptop Refurbishment
- Electronics Recycling
- Corporate Equipment Buy-Back
- Technology Recovery Solutions

---

## Accepted Equipment

We currently accept a wide range of electronic equipment, including:

- Laptops
- Desktop Computers
- Servers
- Network Equipment
- Printers & Multifunction Devices
- UPS Systems
- Hard Drives & SSDs
- Cables & Accessories
- Monitors
- Computer Components

## Mission

To reduce electronic waste through responsible collection, refurbishment, reuse, and recycling while providing secure and sustainable technology disposal solutions for businesses and individuals.
